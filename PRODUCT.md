# GrtBlog Product Context

## Product

GrtBlog is a creator-first blog and content infrastructure platform. It combines a fast, static-first public reading experience with real-time updates, rich personal publishing formats, federation-oriented social features, and a complete administration workspace.

## Register

product

Design serves two connected jobs: helping readers feel welcome and absorbed in a creator's work, and helping creators publish, moderate, understand, and grow that work with confidence. Public content is editorial and personal; the admin surface is focused and operational; both should feel like parts of the same calm, human product rather than separate templates.

## Platform

web

## Target Users

- Readers who want a comfortable, fast, expressive place to discover and follow long-form writing, notes, thoughts, timelines, and social links.
- Independent creators who want ownership of their content, a distinctive personal presence, and reliable publishing infrastructure without sacrificing performance.
- Site operators who manage content, comments, analytics, federation, links, and system settings through the admin workspace.

## Product Purpose

Make personal publishing feel like a living place rather than an isolated archive: quick to open, pleasant to read, easy to maintain, and open to conversation beyond one site.

## Brand Personality

- Warm and personal without becoming cute or sentimental.
- Thoughtful and editorial without feeling precious or slow.
- Technically capable without making infrastructure the visual subject.
- Welcoming, trustworthy, and quietly distinctive.

## Strategic Principles

1. **Human first, infrastructure second.** Technology should enable presence, speed, and continuity without competing with the author's voice.
2. **Reading is a mode, not a component.** Protect focus with clear hierarchy, comfortable measure, deliberate rhythm, and restrained interaction.
3. **One system, different densities.** Public pages can breathe; admin workflows can be denser, but shared typography, color semantics, shape language, and motion should make them recognizably GrtBlog.
4. **Warmth through detail.** Use material, type, microcopy, imagery, and small moments of responsiveness to create personality; avoid decoration without a job.
5. **Fast and resilient by default.** Preserve static-first delivery, accessible states, responsive behavior, and graceful degradation as design requirements.
6. **Conversation is part of publishing.** Comments, moments, federation, friend links, notifications, and realtime updates should feel connected to the content model rather than bolted on.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS dashboards with interchangeable blue gradients, excessive cards, and decorative metrics.
- Overly polished editorial sites that prioritize art direction over scanability, accessibility, or loading performance.
- Social feeds that flatten thoughtful writing into an endless stream of engagement signals.
- Dark-mode-first developer tools that feel cold, dense, or intimidating to readers.
- UI kits where public pages and admin screens use unrelated visual vocabularies.

## Content and Voice

Use plain, direct, considerate language. Let the author's voice lead on public content, while system copy stays concise and reassuring. Explain unfamiliar federation, realtime, moderation, and publishing states instead of assuming technical knowledge. Prefer specific empty-state guidance and human error recovery over terse status labels.

## Design Priorities

1. Create a coherent visual language across the public blog, creator workflows, and admin dashboard.
2. Make reading and writing comfortable on small screens as well as wide screens.
3. Give system states, moderation actions, and realtime updates clear, calm feedback.
4. Preserve room for distinctive author identity within a dependable GrtBlog shell.

## Follow-up Opportunities

- Audit the public reading shell for hierarchy, responsive rhythm, and navigation clarity.
- Polish shared tokens and component states so public and admin surfaces converge without losing their different densities.
- Harden loading, empty, error, and realtime-disconnected states across content and management flows.
